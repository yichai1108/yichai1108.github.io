CN224299790U

A Long-Span Continuous Beam Structure at Bridge Joints

Handong Shu; Yiwen Zhang; Nate Yichai HUANG; Songtao Gu; Qiushi Duan

This structure combines double-wall piers, lower crossbeams, longitudinal beams, diagonal bracing, and a top support beam. Sloped drainage grooves in a rubber diversion block rapidly direct leakage away from beam ends, bearings, and cap beams, reducing freeze–thaw and salt-freeze deterioration through a simple, low-cost configuration.

CN119062015A

A Method for Enhancing the Durability of the Debonding Layer of Concrete Expansion Joints

Yufei Liang; Nate Yichai HUANG; Wei He; Rui Zhang; Xun Zhang; Fang Zhang; Mingzhi Xie

The method uses ultra-high-performance concrete above strength grade C120 as the base layer and an integrated PTFE friction layer with an array of circular projections. The configuration increases friction at the PTFE–UHPC interface and improves the durability and overall performance of the debonding layer under loading and environmental exposure.

CN223005568U

A Mechanical Sensor for Monitoring the Full Life Cycle of Bridge Expansion Joints

Nate Yichai HUANG; Mingyang Ji; Handong Shu; Jiayi Huang; Yueshan Hua; Wei He; Rui Zhang; Xun Zhang; Fang Zhang

The modular installation system secures a crack meter, accelerometer, and embedded concrete strain gauge through locking rods, frames, and protective stops. The integrated sensing arrangement improves data consistency and measurement accuracy for full-lifecycle bridge health assessment while simplifying later sensor maintenance.

An Assembly Structure for Bridge Concrete Contraction Joints

Nate Yichai HUANG; Zhan Yang; Handong Shu; Lingyu Yang; Mingyang Ji; Wei He; Rui Zhang; Xun Zhang; Fang Zhang

A protective plate shields the rubber seal from vehicle vibration, debris, and other direct impacts, reducing wear and damage. Guide blocks allow the protective plate to slide within fixed seats so that the protection system moves synchronously with the edge beams during joint movement.

CN223074582U

A Honeycomb Bridge-Joint Structure

Yufei Liang; Nate Yichai HUANG; Songtao Gu; Handong Shu; Wei He; Rui Zhang; Xun Zhang; Fang Zhang

A removable, wave-shaped rubber waterstop connects to the beam through matching seats and snap-fit blocks. The geometry accommodates joint deformation and displacement while preventing water infiltration, helping protect reinforcement and concrete from corrosion and cracking and extending structural service life.

CN119259415A

A Spraying Method for an Environmentally Friendly Vibration- and Noise-Reducing Polyurethane Replacement Material for Bridge Expansion Joints

Handong Shu; Nate Yichai HUANG; Mingxin Xu; Yufei Liang; Yiwen Zhang; Jiayi Li; Wei He; Rui Zhang; Xun Zhang; Fang Zhang; Mingzhi Xie

After cleaning and leveling the joint substrate to within a 3 mm tolerance, an environmentally friendly foamed material is sprayed uniformly to form a vibration- and noise-reducing layer. The material combines strong mechanical and thermal stability with low environmental impact, disperses traffic vibration and noise, improves ride comfort, and reduces maintenance demand.
