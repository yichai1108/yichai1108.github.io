Auditing Risk-Neutral Generative Models for Option Markets

Developing a framework for auditing diffusion-based SPX option-surface generators and risk-neutral pricing kernels, with emphasis on model reliability and risk-premium identification.

  • Built an end-to-end pipeline using 47.4 million SPX option quotes from OptionMetrics and Heston ground-truth simulations.
  • Disentangles surface representation error, finite-sample entropy bias, and generator misspecification.
  • Evaluates predictive calibration, no-arbitrage consistency, and out-of-sample pricing-kernel validity against autoregressive and Gaussian benchmarks.

Wildfire-Induced Cellular Network Resilience via Monte Carlo–Driven Graph Neural Surrogates

A physics- and graph-informed framework for forecasting time-dependent cellular service outages under joint wildfire and public-safety power shutoff scenarios.

  • Infers cellular network topology from public cell-site data and simulates fire damage, grid loss, backup-power depletion, and disconnection.
  • Combines U-Net and temporal graph neural networks to estimate component failure and probabilistic coverage.
  • Measures population-weighted service loss using hazard, infrastructure, and U.S. Census data.

Anchor-Consistent Conditional Residual Diffusion for Stochastic 3D Micro-CT Completion

Developed a native-resolution conditional diffusion model for reconstructing missing slices in sparsely sampled, multi-phase GGBS CT volumes.

  • Introduces a distance-conditioned residual formulation that models nonlinear structure while keeping measured anchor slices exact.
  • Uses a factorized 3D U-Net with z-axis attention and a multi-objective training loss.
  • Validates morphology, topology, local texture, and cross-slice continuity on held-out specimens.

University of Leeds SWJTU

Deep Learning for Nonlinear Regular-Wave Loads on Dual Cylinders

Built a physics-informed U-Net for spatiotemporal pressure and velocity fields around dual cylinders under fifth-order nonlinear Stokes waves. The workflow combines 3D RANS and k–ω SST simulations with standardized 256×256 multi-channel tensors, reaching a validation MAE of 0.72% while accelerating inference over conventional CFD.
