I Graduated with First Class Honours!

Leeds, United Kingdom · University of Leeds and Southwest Jiaotong University

I am incredibly proud to share that I have officially graduated with the First Class Honours BEng degree in Civil Engineering with Transport from the University of Leeds and Southwest Jiaotong University.

Looking back, this journey was about much more than mastering engineering principles. It was a transformative chapter of personal growth.

As a freshman transitioning to Western education, I was conditioned by my traditional Chinese education to always default to extreme humility. When I tried to attribute a high score to luck, a professor at the University of Leeds firmly corrected me: I had earned it, and there was no need to be humble. This profound cultural shock taught me that genuine confidence originates from authentic self-recognition. Empowered by this newfound self-assurance, it was a true privilege to represent both Southwest Jiaotong University and the University of Leeds in international competitions and achieve top placements.

I want to express my heartiest gratitude to my family, professors, mentors, and friends at both universities. Thank you for being my unwavering anchor, broadening my horizons, and supporting my dreams every single step of the way.

Next, I will relocate to California to begin my Master of Science in Systems Engineering at the University of California, Berkeley. I am eager to apply this technical and personal foundation to the next level of complex engineering challenges.

Forever Leeds. Go Bears.
