News · October 2024

Leading Innovation from Research to Reality

Shanghai, China · National Silver Award at the 2024 China International College Students’ Innovation Competition

I am deeply honored to have served as the team leader of our award-winning project at the 2024 China International College Students’ Innovation Competition (Main Track), where our team received the National Silver Award, ranking among the top 0.01% of more than 5.14 million entries.

This achievement was made possible through the collective efforts of an exceptional 22-member research team, bringing together two academicians, five professors, and fifteen students. I had the privilege of working closely with distinguished academic mentors while leading my fellow students through the research, development, testing, and commercialization of our innovative bridge engineering solution.

Our project focused on addressing the long-standing limitations of traditional steel bridge expansion joints, including excessive traffic noise, frequent maintenance requirements, and potential safety concerns. To overcome these challenges, we developed an innovative bridge expansion plate using high-toughness, high-durability concrete, designed to improve structural performance, reduce maintenance demands, and enhance the long-term reliability of bridge infrastructure.

Through extensive laboratory testing and close collaboration with industry partners, we successfully validated our proposed design and advanced the technology toward practical implementation. Our solution was subsequently applied in Chengdu, China, demonstrating its potential to address real-world infrastructure challenges.

Beyond the competition, one of our most meaningful accomplishments was securing RMB 1 million in intended orders, reflecting industry interest in the commercial potential of our technology and representing an important step toward translating academic research into engineering practice.

I am sincerely grateful to the academicians and professors who generously shared their expertise and guidance throughout this journey, as well as to every student teammate whose dedication, creativity, and perseverance made this achievement possible.

For me, this experience was about much more than winning a national award. It reinforced my belief that meaningful engineering innovation emerges when academic research, interdisciplinary collaboration, and real-world needs come together. I am proud of what we accomplished as a team and look forward to continuing to develop engineering solutions that make infrastructure safer, more resilient, and more sustainable.
